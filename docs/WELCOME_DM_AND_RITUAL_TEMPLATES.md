# TWO welcome DM sequence + weekly-ritual announcement templates

Status: **DRAFT, docs only** — pending CPO acceptance. No live-guild writes, no bot action, no member contact on this card.

Seven-day metric: content used in one pilot ritual within 7d of acceptance, or revised.

## 0. Consent guard (read first)

The week-1 ritual kit bars unsolicited DMs and mass messaging. This sequence is **opt-in only**: DMs go only to members who accepted the rules **and** either reacted to the in-server welcome prompt or explicitly asked for onboarding help. Anything else stays in-server. If CPO wants broader DM coverage, that is a separate decision — this doc does not authorize it.

Tone: warm, plain, short. Humans first; any bot send is labeled as the TWO bot. Every message offers a human to talk to. No guilt, no streak pressure, no fake urgency.

## 1. Placeholders (fill at send time, never hardcode)

`[NAME]` · `[SERVER-NAME]` · `[CHANNEL:#general]` · `[CHANNEL:#events]` · `[CHANNEL:#intros]`
`[TOPIC]` · `[DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE]` (wall-clock) · `[DURATION]` · `[HOST]` · `[RSVP-ACTION — canonical only]`
`[PARTICIPATION-PROMPT]` · `[WELCOME-LINK]` (current invite/rules link from the announcements post)

## 2. Welcome DM sequence (4 messages, opt-in only)

Send 1 immediately on opt-in; 2 after 24h only if no first message yet; 3 after 72h only if still no first message; 4 after first message or day 7. Stop the sequence the moment the member posts or asks to stop. Never more than one DM in 24h.

### DM 1 — Welcome (on opt-in)

> Hi [NAME]! Welcome to [SERVER-NAME] — glad you're here. 🌱
>
> I'm the TWO bot (automated, a human reads replies). Three quick things:
> 1. Say hi in [CHANNEL:#intros] — handle + one thing you're into.
> 2. Skim the rules if you haven't yet — that's what fully unlocks the server.
> 3. Our weekly hangout is [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE] in [CHANNEL:#events] — come as you are, no prep.
>
> Reply here any time and a human will pick it up. What's one thing you'd like to get out of TWO?

### DM 2 — Gentle nudge (24h later, only if no first message)

> Quick hello again, [NAME] — no pressure, just making sure you found your footing. 🙂
>
> Easiest first step: drop a hi in [CHANNEL:#intros]. Someone usually answers fast — that's kind of our thing.
>
> Stuck on anything? Reply here and a human helps. Want me to stop checking in? Say "stop" and I will, no hard feelings.

### DM 3 — The ritual invite (72h later, only if still no first message)

> Last scheduled check-in, [NAME] — then I'll leave you in peace. 💛
>
> This week's hangout: **[TOPIC]**, [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE] ([DURATION]) in [CHANNEL:#events], hosted by [HOST]. First-timers just listen if they like — the only question is "[PARTICIPATION-PROMPT]".
>
> To join: [RSVP-ACTION]. Or just show up. Hope to see you — and "stop" still works any time.

### DM 4 — After first contact (post-first-message, or day 7)

> You're in, [NAME]! 🎉 Saw your message — that's the hardest part done.
>
> What's next, pick any: join the weekly hangout ([DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE], [CHANNEL:#events]), browse [CHANNEL:#general], or lurk — lurking is a valid strategy.
>
> This is my last planned message. The humans are in the channels whenever you need them. Have fun!

## 3. Weekly-ritual announcement templates (in-server)

### 3a. Announcement (T-72h, one post, one channel)

> 📣 **Weekly hangout — [TOPIC]**
> 🗓 [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE] · ⏱ [DURATION] · 📍 [CHANNEL:#events] · hosted by [HOST]
>
> This week: [ONE-SENTENCE HOOK — what we'll do, not a lecture].
> First-timers welcome — no prep, listening counts. The opening question: "[PARTICIPATION-PROMPT]".
>
> RSVP here: [RSVP-ACTION]. See you there! 🌱

### 3b. Reminder (T-24h, only to RSVP-yes / "remind me" replies)

> ⏰ Reminder: **[TOPIC]** is tomorrow, [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE] in [CHANNEL:#events].
>
> Bring nothing but yourself. Opening question: "[PARTICIPATION-PROMPT]". ([HOST] hosting — reply here if you can't make it after all.)

### 3c. Recap (within 48h after, posted either way — held or cancelled)

> 📝 **Hangout recap — [TOPIC]**
> Thanks to everyone who came! [2–3 aggregate takeaways — themes only, no verbatim quotes without consent.]
>
> Next week: same slot, [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE]. Topic + RSVP land in [CHANNEL:#events] soon. Missed it? You're caught up — see you next time. 💛

### 3d. Cancellation (fewer than 3 RSVPs at T-24h go/no-go)

> This week's hangout is cancelled ([N] RSVPs, needed 3). Next try: [DAY, TIME + TIMEZONE]. Thanks to those who RSVP'd — you're the reason we keep the slot. 🌱
