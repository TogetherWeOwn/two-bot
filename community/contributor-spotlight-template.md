# Contributor spotlight template (pack 3)

> DRAFT ONLY — copy template, no post, no DM, no bot change. For CPO copy
> approval. Human-posted: a human fills the placeholders, gets the member's
> OK, and posts. No live-guild action in this slice.

| | |
|---|---|
| Intended channel | #announcements or landing channel (CPO picks per edition) |
| Intended use | Irregular (max 1/week) member shout-out: thank contributors, model the culture |
| Cadence guard | Never adjacent to a Sunday Squad announcement day; spotlight bows to the ritual |

## Rules (privacy + tone, non-negotiable)

1. **Consent first:** the featured member says yes to being featured *and* to the
   final text before anything posts. A "yes to the idea" is not a yes to the copy.
   Per `docs/PRIVACY.md`, we store IDs and timestamps, never message content —
   so write the spotlight from what you observed with permission, never by
   quoting DMs or pulling logs.
2. **No DMs to arrange it:** ask in the open (reply in the channel where they
   helped) or not at all. The bot never DMs; neither does this template's workflow.
3. **Name the deed, not the person:** what they did, what it unlocked for others,
   how someone else can do it too. No ranks, no leaderboards, no "top contributor".
4. **One ask, softly:** every edition ends with the same low-bar invitation
   (see template). No sign-up, no form, no thread required.

## Paste-ready template

```text
🌟 **Contributor spotlight: {display_name}**

{one_or_two_sentences_on_what_they_did — e.g. "Ran the party fill while the host's mic died, and got three first-timers into their first show."}

What that unlocked: {who_benefited_and_how — e.g. "Sunday kept rolling with zero dead air, and the newcomers stayed for the whole hour."}

Want in? Just do the thing you already do — hop in early, grab a newcomer, explain one round. That's the whole job. Thanks, {display_name}. 💛

— posted with {display_name}'s OK
```

## Worked example (fictional, delete before posting)

🌟 **Contributor spotlight: Sam**
