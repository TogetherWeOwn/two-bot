## Post 1 — Welcome (join day)

```text
Hey @member — glad you're here. 💛

TWO is a small gaming crew with one ritual: Sunday Squad, every Sunday at 8pm Eastern in the Lobby voice room. One hour of Fall Guys, free on every platform, zero skill required.

You don't need to sign up or say anything first — just hop into voice whenever you see people in there. Haven't got the game? Come anyway, there's always something we can play in the room itself.

Stick around, say hi once, and you're one of us. See you Sunday. 👑
```

*Word count: 91. No links; channel named in plain words so the copy works wherever a human posts it.*

## Post 2 — How contributing works (day 2–3)

```text
Quick note on how contributing works here: there's no application, no role to earn first, no sign-up sheet.

Contributing is just the thing you already do — hop in a few minutes early, grab a newcomer, explain one round. Cover the doors while the host's mic dies. Stay to the end so nobody plays alone.

Nobody keeps score and there's no leaderboard. If you helped someone have a good hour, that's the whole job. And if you're not sure what to do, just ask in the open — someone will point you at the nearest small thing.
```

*Word count: 97. No links; mirrors the spotlight template's "name the deed" culture (`community/contributor-spotlight-template.md`).*

## Post 3 — Three first tasks, pick one (day 6–7)

```text
Want a first task? Pick whichever one sounds like you — doing one of them once counts as contributing:

1. Doors — be in the Lobby voice room 5 minutes before Sunday Squad and pull arrivals straight into the party so nobody sits in silence.
2. Buddy — find one first-timer, explain one round, check they got into a show.
3. Closer — stay to the last round and say goodnight by name to whoever's left.

That's it. No form, no thread, no report. Do one, and thank you — you're what keeps Sunday rolling.
```

*Word count: 95. No links; tasks mirror the run-of-show beats in `community/sunday-squad-event-2026-10-18.md` (doors / party fill / wrap).*
