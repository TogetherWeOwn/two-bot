# TWO member-growth content pack: event promo + engagement prompts

> DRAFT ONLY — copy templates, no post, no DM, no bot change, nothing
> scheduled. For CPO copy approval; a human fills placeholders and posts
> each item at its slot. No live-guild action in this slice.

| | |
|---|---|
| Series | Sunday Squad, Sundays 20:00–21:00 America/New_York, 🔊🏠 Lobby voice |
| Intended channels | Landing/announcements for promo; general/intro/media for prompts (CPO picks per item) |
| Cadence guards | Promo bows to nothing — it IS the ritual. Engagement prompts never post on a Sunday Squad announcement day; max 2 prompts/week; showcase/spotlight formats keep their own guards |

Guards across every item: no @everyone/@here; no DMs to round people up;
no message-content quotes and no member names without their OK (per
`docs/PRIVACY.md` we store IDs and timestamps, never message text); heads
logged as-is, never inflated. Slot stays wall-clock in a named timezone
per `docs/ANCHOR_EVENT.md`.

## Part A — event-promo templates (pre / during / post)

### A1. Pre-event teaser (Thu/Fri before, general or landing channel)

Post by: 18:00 ET Thu or Fri. Owner: CM drafts; CPO approves; human posts.

```text
🎮 **Sunday Squad — this Sunday, 8pm Eastern**

One hour of Fall Guys in the 🔊🏠 Lobby. Free, no sign-up, no experience needed — every week starts from zero, so first-timers and returners get the same welcome.

Got a friend who's been lurking? This is the week to drag them in. See you Sunday. 👑
```

Why this copy: midweek teaser catches planners without spoiling the
Sunday announcement; "every week starts from zero" is the standing
re-invitation for people who missed last week; ends on bring-a-friend,
the cheapest growth lever we have.

### A2. Pre-event announcement (morning of the event, landing channel)

Post by: 10:00 ET Sunday. Owner: CM drafts; CPO approves; human host posts.

```text
👑 **Sunday Squad is tonight — 8pm Eastern, 🔊🏠 Lobby**

Same deal: an hour of Fall Guys, no sign-up, no experience needed. Haven't got it installed? There's something we can play right there in the room.

Just drop into Lobby voice at 8. Bring a friend, or just bring yourself. 💛
```

Why this copy: short enough for a phone lock-screen notification; repeats
the three friction removers (free, no sign-up, come-as-you-are) verbatim
every week so the series reads as one ritual, not a relaunch.

### A3. During-event doors-open ping (event start, landing channel or event chat)

Post at: 20:00 ET Sunday, when the host opens the room. Owner: human host posts; no approval needed (fixed text).

```text
🔴 **Doors are open — Sunday Squad is live in 🔊🏠 Lobby**

Hop in now, we're filling the first party. Late? Come anyway — we re-fill every round. 🎮
```

Why this copy: fixed text the host can paste in five seconds; "re-fill
every round" kills the #1 late-joiner fear (walking into a locked game).
Never @everyone — the Sunday announcement already did the notifying.

### A4. Post-event recap (within 48h, announcements channel)

Post by: within 48h of the event. Owner: human host fills the recap line; CM owns the record.

```text
🎮 **Sunday Squad recap**

{recap_line — e.g. "Six of us, one crown, and a first-timer who nearly stole the final."} Thanks to everyone who dropped in — and to {newcomer_names | "the new faces"} for joining.

Same time next week: Sunday 8pm Eastern in the 🔊🏠 Lobby. Bring a friend — it's free and there's nothing to be rusty at.
```

Host fill-in rules: heads counted at doors, logged as-is; newcomer names
only with their separate recap OK, otherwise the generic line; never
invent attendance, quotes, or activity. Quiet or cancelled weeks reported
as-is ("no run tonight — back Sunday") with no substitute drafted same-day.

### A5. Post-event miss-you nudge (Tue/Wed after, general channel)

Post by: 18:00 ET Tue or Wed. Owner: CM drafts; CPO approves; human posts.
Skip this item on weeks the recap already names newcomers (avoid double-pinging the same people).

```text
💛 **Missed Sunday? You missed nothing.**

Every Sunday Squad starts from zero — no catch-up, no inside jokes you had to be there for. Next run: Sunday 8pm Eastern, 🔊🏠 Lobby. First-timers get the full welcome. 🎮
```

Why this copy: converts absence into an invitation instead of guilt;
pairs with the recap (recap celebrates who came, this re-invites who
didn't) without naming or numbering absentees.

## Part B — 10 server engagement prompts

Each prompt: paste-ready copy, intended channel, cadence. All human-posted.
None requires sign-up, a form, or a thread. Prompts 1–10 in any order, max
2/week, never on a Sunday announcement day.

### B1. Intro icebreaker (intro channel, on demand for new joins)

```text
👋 **New here? Start with one line.**

Tell us: what game brought you here, and what's one game you've always wanted to try? No essays — a fragment each is perfect.
```

### B2. Clip / moment call (media channel, max 1/week)

```text
🎬 **Clip it.**

Funniest, clutchest, or most chaotic thing you pulled off this week — in any game. Drop the clip (or just describe it) in this channel. Best story gets absolutely nothing except our full respect. 👏
```

### B3. Unpopular gaming opinion (general channel, max 1/fortnight)

```text
🌶️ **Unpopular gaming opinion.**

Drop yours. One rule: defend it in one sentence. I'll start: {host_seeds_with_their_own_first — e.g. "Fall Guys lobbies are better with randoms than a full stack."}
```

Host rule: the poster seeds their own opinion first — never an empty
provocation. If replies heat up, steer to games, never to people.

### B4. Play-with-me board (general or LFG channel, weekly, non-Sunday)

```text
🎮 **Who's playing what this week?**

Drop the game + when you're usually on. Goal: find one person whose hours overlap yours and play one round together. That's the whole quest.
```

### B5. First-timer tips (general channel, week after any event with newcomers)

```text
🧭 **Veterans: one tip for a first-timer.**

You remember your first Sunday Squad — what do you wish someone had told you before round one? One tip each. Newcomers: steal all of them.
```

### B6. Show-your-setup / battlestation lite (media channel, max 1/month)

```text
🖥️ **Show your setup.**

Desk, couch, handheld-in-bed — all battlestations are valid. Photo or one-line description. No gear-shaming; the 2014 laptop that still runs is elite.
```

### B7. Game-picker poll (general channel, when the host wants a weeknight game)

```text
🗳️ **Pick the weeknight game.**

{option_A} vs {option_B} — react with 1️⃣ or 2️⃣. Free-to-play only, runs on a potato preferred. Poll closes {deadline — e.g. "Thu 6pm ET"}. Majority wins, no recounts.
```

Host rule: both options must be genuinely playable (free, low-spec) so
the vote never strands newcomers. Report the result and the play date in
the same channel.

### B8. Shout-out thread (general channel, max 1/fortnight)

```text
💛 **Shout-out thread.**

Tag someone who made your week better — carried a round, explained a mechanic, or just kept voice chat fun. One line on what they did. (Shout-outs welcome any time; this thread just reminds us.)
```

Privacy rule: tag from shared play only; no DM content quoted. Anyone can
ask to be left out of future threads, no questions asked.

### B9. Lurk-no-more roll call (general channel, max 1/month)

```text
👀 **Lurkers, this one's for you.**

Been reading but not typing? React with 👋 so we know you're alive — no follow-up required, no spotlight, no "now tell us about yourself." Lurking is a valid playstyle; we just like knowing you're out there.
```

Why this copy: the explicit no-follow-up promise is the whole mechanic —
it converts invisible members to visible ones at zero social cost.

### B10. Event-idea box (general channel, max 1/month)

```text
💡 **What should we run next?**

One extra event alongside Sunday Squad — what would get YOU to show up? Game + format in one line (e.g. "Jackbox night, teams of 4"). Wildest viable idea gets a pilot date.
```

Host rule: reply to every serious suggestion once (even "not yet, and
here's why"); the box dies the first time ideas go unanswered.
